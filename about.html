About Oscyra Solutions

Building privacy-first technology for a better digital future

Who We Are

Oscyra Solutions is an innovation lab based in Svenarum, Sweden, dedicated to creating privacy-first software solutions that challenge the status quo of modern technology. We believe that powerful software doesn't have to compromise user privacy, and that innovative design can deliver both exceptional performance and complete data protection.

Founded with a vision to create alternatives to mainstream tech giants, we focus on building tools that respect users, prioritize transparency, and deliver real value without hidden costs or data exploitation.

Our Mission

To develop innovative, privacy-respecting software solutions that empower users with complete control over their data while delivering exceptional performance and user experience. We're committed to building technology that serves people, not advertisers.

Our Core Values

🔐 Privacy First

User privacy is not negotiable. We build GDPR-compliant solutions with zero tracking, no profiling, and complete transparency.

⚡ Performance Matters

Fast, efficient, and optimized. We believe great software should be lightning-quick without sacrificing functionality.

🌍 Open & Transparent

We embrace open source principles and believe in transparency. Our code and practices are open for scrutiny.

🇸🇪 Local Focus

Built in Sweden, for Sweden first. We understand local needs and create solutions tailored for Swedish users.

🚀 Innovation Driven

We push boundaries and explore new possibilities in software design, always looking for better ways to solve problems.

💎 Quality Over Quantity

We focus on building fewer, better products rather than rushing to market with half-baked solutions.

Our Projects

Klar Search Engine

A privacy-first search ecosystem designed specifically for Swedish users. Klar combines a lightweight desktop browser with a powerful search engine backend, delivering fast, relevant, ad-free search results without any tracking or profiling. With GDPR compliance at its core, Klar proves that you don't need to sacrifice privacy for functionality.

Dynamic OS

A custom operating system built from the ground up with modern kernel architecture, focusing on performance and security. Dynamic OS represents our vision of what an operating system should be—fast, secure, and completely transparent.

Project AION

An AI-driven platform leveraging neural networks for intelligent automation and decision-making in cybersecurity applications. AION explores the intersection of artificial intelligence and security, building systems that can learn and adapt while maintaining user privacy.

Why We Build What We Build

The modern internet has strayed from its original promise of open, free information exchange. Instead, we're surrounded by platforms that track every click, profile every user, and monetize personal data. Search engines show ads disguised as results. Browsers collect browsing history. "Free" services come at the hidden cost of privacy.

We believe there's a better way. Technology can be powerful, innovative, and user-friendly without exploiting personal data. At Oscyra Solutions, we're proving that privacy-first software isn't just possible—it's superior.

Looking Ahead

We're just getting started. Each project we build brings us closer to our vision of a digital ecosystem where privacy is default, performance is exceptional, and users are in control. Whether you're a Swedish user looking for a private search alternative, a developer interested in contributing, or simply someone who believes in our mission, we invite you to join us on this journey.
