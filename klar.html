What is Klar?

Klar is a complete privacy-first search ecosystem designed specifically for Sweden. Unlike traditional search engines that track your every move, Klar offers a complete alternative consisting of two components:

CLIENT-SIDE

Klar Browser

The desktop/mobile application that users download and install. Think of it like Chrome or Firefox, but specialized exclusively for private search.

  • Download and install on your device
  • Simple, clean search interface
  • Connects to Klar Search Engine
  • No cookies, no tracking, no ads
  • Completely free and open source
SERVER-SIDE

Klar Search Engine (KSE)

The powerful backend server infrastructure that processes search queries and returns results. Run by Oscyra Solutions.

  • Indexes 2.8+ million Swedish pages
  • Crawls 2,543 Swedish domains
  • Processes queries in < 500ms
  • Never stores user data
  • GDPR-compliant by design

How They Work Together

Klar = Privacy + Speed + Relevance (Swedish)

Users download Klar Browser (the client), type their search, and it's sent securely to Klar Search Engine (the server) which returns fast, relevant results — all without storing any information about you. No tracking, no profiling, no data collection.

Why Klar is Different

The Problem with Traditional Search Engines

Google, Bing, and other mainstream search engines:

❌ Track Everything

Every search you make is logged, stored, and analyzed to build a detailed profile about you.

❌ Sell Your Data

Your search history, browsing patterns, and personal interests are monetized and sold to advertisers.

❌ Show Ads First

Search results are cluttered with ads, sponsored content, and SEO-gamed pages instead of relevant answers.

❌ Profile Your Behavior

Cookies, trackers, and fingerprinting follow you across the web, building an invasive digital profile.

The Klar Solution

Klar takes a completely different approach:

✅ Zero Tracking

Klar never tracks your searches, browsing behavior, or personal information. What you search is yours alone.

✅ No Data Collection

We don't store logs, cookies, or user data. Every search is anonymous and leaves no trace.

✅ 100% Ad-Free

Pure search results with no ads, no sponsored links, no SEO manipulation. Just relevant answers.

✅ GDPR-Compliant

Built from the ground up to respect privacy laws. Full GDPR compliance with complete transparency.

✅ Swedish-Focused

Optimized specifically for Swedish users, indexing Swedish domains and local sources for better relevance.

✅ Lightning Fast

Search results in under 500ms with a clean, distraction-free interface that just works.
